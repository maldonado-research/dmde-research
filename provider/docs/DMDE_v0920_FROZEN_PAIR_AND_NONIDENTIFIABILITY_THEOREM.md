# Frozen-pair source theorem and energy-only non-identifiability

## Rank-one frozen-pair source

For blind card `i`, define

\[
A_i=B_{\mu\mu,i}Y_{0,i}.
\]

The two frozen cards share the same lifetime, muon mass, time dependence, energy coordinate, and Michel kernels. Their complete pre-transport spectral sources therefore satisfy

\[
\mathbf S_i(t,x)=A_i\frac{e^{-t/\tau}}{\tau}
\left(F_e,F_e,F_\mu,F_\mu,0,0\right).
\]

The two-card source matrix has rank one. Pointwise, for every nonzero source component,

\[
\frac{\mathbf S_{8M2K}}{\mathbf S_{4Q7N}}
=\frac{B_{8M2K}Y_{8M2K}}{B_{4Q7N}Y_{4Q7N}}
=1.0086922376507683.
\]

Every linear source functional—including every source moment and source-level Born capture sentinel—must scale by the same ratio.

The frozen energy-source ratios are

| Quantity, `8M2K/4Q7N` | Ratio |
|---|---:|
| Complete neutrino spectrum and neutrino energy | 1.008692237650768 |
| Electromagnetic energy | 1.000003985978959 |
| Parent rest-energy abundance | 1.001960485305408 |
| Lifetime | 1 |

Thus the pair is an electromagnetic common-mode comparison to about four parts per million while its neutrino source differs by `0.8692238%`. Of the total injected-energy difference, `99.8424686%` is neutrino and `0.1575314%` electromagnetic.

These are source-level conformance identities. Collisions, oscillations, expansion, weak-rate feedback, and nuclear evolution are nonlinear, so the ratios must not be imposed on final `Y_p`, D/H, or `N_eff`.

## Energy-only non-identifiability

The electron Michel spectrum and a monoenergetic surrogate can have exactly the same neutrino number and total energy while producing different charged-current weak functionals.

For

\[
F_e(x)=12x^2(1-x),\qquad E_\star=m_\mu/2,
\]

the Michel source has

\[
\langle E\rangle=\frac35E_\star,qquad
\langle E^2\rangle=\frac25E_\star^2.
\]

Compare it with

\[
g_\delta(E)=\delta\!\left(E-\frac35E_\star\right).
\]

Both sources have unit number and the same total energy. Nevertheless, direct finite-electron-mass Born quadrature gives:

| Phase-space functional | Michel/mono − 1 |
|---|---:|
| `nu_e + n -> p + e-` | +10.2582% |
| `anti-nu_e + p -> n + e+` | +12.0782% |

This is an existence proof that number plus total energy does not determine the weak rate. It is not a prediction of a 10–12% change in any abundance. It proves why an energy-only return cannot replace spectral transport without an explicit equivalence demonstration.

With the frozen cutoff `L=18`, the active completeness is

\[
C_{18}=1-e^{-18}=0.9999999847700203.
\]

The pair produces four neutrinos with total mean energy

\[
E_{\nu,{\rm pair}}=\frac{13}{10}m_\mu=137.35588815\ {m MeV},
\]

and the mean over all four neutrinos is

\[
\langle E_\nu\rangle_{\rm all}=\frac{13m_\mu}{40}=34.3389720375\ {m MeV}.
\]

These exact identities supply independent checks of the actual source-RHS number and energy closure.
