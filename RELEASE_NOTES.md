# DMDE v0.9.20 — GitHub Publication Notes

This GitHub release republishes the public DMDE v0.9.20 research artifacts and adds a browsable repository, citation metadata, a plain-language overview, and reproducibility instructions.

**Archival research record:** [10.5281/zenodo.22399940](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22399940).

**Status:** working hypothesis; methods/software checkpoint; not peer reviewed. No validated production helium, deuterium, or effective-neutrino-number predictions, and no independent backend replication are supplied.

## In More Basic Terms

The release improves the checks used to test a hypothesis about dark matter and dark energy. It keeps two proposed particle sources fixed and requires a future calculation to show that it actually follows their particles through neutrino evolution, neutron–proton reactions, and early element formation. It also explains how close the sources are and how precisely their eventual outputs would need to be compared.

These improvements make the proposed test more inspectable. They do not establish that the hypothesis describes nature or unifies physics.
