# Focused DMDE literature and backend check

Observed 2026-10-02 UTC (2026-10-01 America/Los_Angeles). Scientific cutoff: **2026-10-01**. This was a focused check of actual arXiv pages/PDFs, the official PDG reviews, the official DESI news index, and a paper-linked public GitHub repository. It was not an exhaustive web survey. No private archive content is reproduced. No external code was executed and no DMDE calculation workflow was changed.

## Most useful new lead: momentum-resolved transport coupled to BBN

**Akita, Escudero, Ihnatenko and Ovchynnikov, “Neutrino Direct Simulation Monte Carlo: Accurate modeling of out-of-equilibrium decaying cosmological relics,” arXiv:2609.31833v1, 25 September 2026.** [Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31833v1), [paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.31833v1).

The full PDF, not only the abstract, was inspected. Section VI describes BBN inputs comprising cosmic time and scale factor versus photon temperature; physical electron-neutrino and antineutrino occupations versus momentum; relic number density; and charged-pion production. It evolves neutron/proton conversion and a nuclear network through deuterium, tritium, helium and lithium/beryllium. Section VIII reports independent quasi-classical Boltzmann comparisons and explicitly cautions that convergence of total neutrino energy density does not ensure convergence of helium/deuterium when high-energy spectral tails drive weak rates. These are the authors' reported validations; this session did not reproduce them.

This is a relevant **candidate for a later DMDE transport/BBN comparison**, not a validated DMDE backend or evidence for the hypothesis. The documented assumptions include equal neutrino and antineutrino occupations and negligible charged-lepton asymmetries. The inspected paper/interface do not establish a ready-made primary muon-decay injection channel. An arbitrary initialized neutrino array is also not equivalent to a time-dependent, energy-conserving muon-decay source.

The paper directly links [maksymovchynnikov/nuDSMC](https://github.com/maksymovchynnikov/nuDSMC). Read-only API checks found main at **438ad2fbb6e3e0f896b061b18c525208be8c9af0**, committed 29 September 2026. Current repository contents supersede the PDF's pending-C++-release statement: optional native libraries are now supplied for Apple Silicon macOS and Windows x86-64. The documented Linux route remains Mathematica with locally compiled helpers; a Linux native library is not offered. No installed Wolfram runtime or ability to run this package was established in this check.

At this exact commit:

- [Native interface](https://github.com/maksymovchynnikov/nuDSMC/blob/438ad2fbb6e3e0f896b061b18c525208be8c9af0/Native-iterator.md) documents neutrino-pair, electron/positron-pair, photon-pair and neutrino/photon decay channels. Primary muon decay is not a documented input.
- [License notices](https://github.com/maksymovchynnikov/nuDSMC/blob/438ad2fbb6e3e0f896b061b18c525208be8c9af0/LICENSES.md) apply BSD-2-Clause to the C++ library only. They assign no new license to Mathematica sources, auxiliary data or bundled BBN files. The GitHub repository-level license field must not be generalized to all files.
- [Python BBN documentation](https://github.com/maksymovchynnikov/nuDSMC/blob/438ad2fbb6e3e0f896b061b18c525208be8c9af0/docs/BBN.md) refers to a separate `mev-universe` project without a public repository URL on that page. It requires five consistent histories (`summary`, pre-evolution, evolution, neutrino spectra and pion production), including actual bin boundaries and preceding thermal evolution. Availability of that separate project and compatibility with DMDE were not established.

A future integration would first need a supported runtime, a licensed/documented interface, the intended muon source with all daughter energy accounted for, a standard-model control, spectral-bin/unit checks, separate neutron-to-proton and proton-to-neutron rate checks, and convergence of both abundances and radiation density. Nothing in this source removes those requirements.

## Weak-rate and observational cross-checks

**Pitrou, Coc, Uzan and Vangioni**, [“Precision big bang nucleosynthesis with improved Helium-4 predictions,” arXiv:1801.08023](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.08023), DOI [10.1016/j.physrep.2018.04.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2018.04.005). Original submission: 23 January 2018; latest retrieved version: **v3, 6 February 2025**. This is the PRIMAT reference, including radiative, finite-mass, finite-temperature, weak-magnetism and plasma corrections with detailed-balance checks. Its quoted historical helium prediction is tied to its inputs and is not a universal present-day target for a differently configured solver.

**Ganguly, Jung and Yun**, [“Consistent N_eff fitting in big bang nucleosynthesis analysis,” arXiv:2507.23354v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.23354v2), 24 April 2026 (originally 31 July 2025). The full PDF supports the warning that reducing radiation through electromagnetic entropy injection changes weak rates as well as expansion. Extrapolating an extra-dark-radiation parameter to negative values with unchanged rates is not physically equivalent. The paper's simplified calculations are not a precision calibration; its appendix explicitly reports residual standard-BBN abundance differences. Implication: keep DMDE's spectral mechanism distinct from a fit using only N_eff.

**Chluba, Cyr, Uwabo-Niibo and Yamaguchi**, [“Neutrino-electron scattering kernels in isotropic media,” arXiv:2607.25436v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25436v1), 28 July 2026. The abstract reports analytic reduction of collision kernels from five to two dimensions and analytic moments. This is a possible method reference, not an implemented or validated improvement to DMDE. Only metadata/abstract were reviewed here.

**Froustey et al.**, [“Impact of non-standard neutrino-electron interactions on Big Bang Nucleosynthesis,” arXiv:2602.23915v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23915v2), 28 July 2026. The abstract reports BBN limits weaker than terrestrial limits for its studied interaction parameters. Adding particle interactions requires laboratory constraints too; cosmological flexibility does not establish viability. Only metadata/abstract reviewed.

**Yeh et al.**, [“The LBT Y_p Project V: Cosmological Implications of a New Determination of Primordial 4He,” arXiv:2601.22239v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22239v1), 29 January 2026. The abstract reports Y_p = 0.2458 ± 0.0013 and consistency with the standard cosmology. This is a candidate updated helium likelihood, not an automatic replacement for an existing checkpoint's observation choice. Its quoted bound on additional neutrino species assumes its specified BBN/CMB model and prior; it cannot simply be applied to arbitrary nonthermal injections. Only metadata/abstract reviewed.

The authoritative [PDG BBN review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-bbang-nucleosynthesis.pdf), Fields/Molaro/Sarkar, revised August 2025, gives neutron lifetime 878.4 ± 0.5 s, recommended observational helium 0.245 ± 0.003, and stresses residual deuterium nuclear-rate uncertainties. The [PDG neutrino review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-neutrinos-in-cosmology.pdf), Lesgourgues/Verde, revised August 2025, describes spectral decoupling, oscillations and plasma corrections behind the standard N_eff ≈ 3.044. These help define controls and uncertainty bookkeeping; they are review sources, not new DMDE measurements.

## Dark energy, structure and lensing

The primary **DESI Collaboration**, [“DESI DR2 Results II: Measurements of Baryon Acoustic Oscillations and Cosmological Constraints,” arXiv:2503.14738v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14738v3), 9 October 2025 (originally 18 March 2025), reports that BAO alone is well described by flat ΛCDM, with a mild parameter tension against CMB. Evolving-dark-energy preference in combined BAO+CMB+supernova analyses ranges from 2.8 to 4.2 sigma depending on the supernova sample. It is not a detection of a specific DMDE mechanism. These values were checked in the actual primary abstract and contextualized using the [PDG dark-energy review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-dark-energy.pdf), Weinberg/White, revised August 2025.

Two newer, directly verified primary-preprint abstracts illustrate the remaining interpretive issue:

- **Sanders et al.**, [“A Unified Tracer Analysis of DESI DR2 Baryon Acoustic Oscillations,” arXiv:2608.27830v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27830v1), 28 August 2026, reports consistency with baseline DR2 and no detected tracer-dependent systematic over 0.8 < z < 1.6; its finer redshift bins reproduce the evolving-equation-of-state preference. That tested range and class of systematic are specific.
- **Watkins, Trueax and Feldman**, [“Consistency of DES and DESI distances and the Standard Cosmological Model,” arXiv:2606.18374v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18374v1), 16 June 2026, argues that a roughly 0.05-mag low-redshift supernova offset drives much of the preference and points to preliminary alternative-anchor data. This is an analysis claim requiring full methodological review, not a settled refutation of DESI.

These papers examine different possible systematics and do not establish either universal failure or definitive confirmation of evolving dark energy. A DMDE background-expansion proposal still needs an explicit conserved stress-energy model, a ΛCDM baseline and a matched-data comparison with parameter penalties. If it is intended to replace dark matter, the [PDG dark-matter review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-dark-matter.pdf), Baudis/Profumo, revised August 2025, emphasizes structure growth, CMB anisotropies and merging-cluster lensing as independent requirements. Passing BBN alone demonstrates none of those later-time properties.
