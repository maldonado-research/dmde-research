# Review conclusion

The authored exact-muon-mass callback, native reference-entropy conversion,
charge-symmetry aliases and actual-time half-open gate are internally coherent
and independently verified for the public diagnostic fixtures. All native
transport, collision and thermodynamic masses are retained by the actual
runtime adapter; the source callback alone owns the frozen muon mass.

The independent real-RHS review checks the actual returned callback samples
using a beta-density representation, then derives the canonical response from
raw same-state source-on/off native RHS vectors. An explicit PMNS matrix
construction predicts the mixed response to `7.81e-16` of peak. The canonical
pre-mixing export is the actual callback capture multiplied by the wrapper's
declared conversion; it is not a directly instrumented native accumulator.
Six exports are three native states aliased under charge symmetry, not six
independently evolved histories.

The source-only zero callback retains the parent background and deposition.
Its photon-temperature derivative differs from the full native-gate-off
control, as expected. Source-on/off time derivatives are identical at the
same state. Source callback calls occur at the predecessor of `18*tau` and
stop at the exact cutoff and successor, including declared x/t combinations
that disagree with an x-only cutoff. Negative times are rejected at the
adapter interface, not evolved.

Independent incomplete-beta moments reproduce all 3,003 source-only scan rows.
At `q=[0.01,40]` and the 1,001 declared x points from 0.1 to 0.2, maximum
relative moment errors are `0.00539153`, `0.00398695` and `0.00202978` for
3,001, 4,001 and 8,001 nodes. Lower-domain loss is at most `3.62e-9` and is
kept distinct from quadrature error. These are sampled-source findings, not
continuous-x bounds or a transport accuracy certificate.

The actual 65-node real-RHS fixture has maximum finite-domain source moment
error **0.0114882**, failing the frozen `0.005` moment ceiling. It still gives
a meaningful local coefficient witness because its actual same-node callback
response is verified. No normalization is applied to repair the moments.

The native unsplit RHS produces tau injection after mixing. Its infinitesimal
per-species response differs from the declared pre-mixing source by
**0.580347** of the source peak. Halving a full mixed step cannot remove the
nonzero leading tau response. The frozen gate's directly compared pre-mixing
RHS is therefore unsuitable for this native unsplit candidate. A production
scheme with an actually consumed source substep may supply a distinct witness;
no universal incompatibility of physical operator splitting is claimed.

After the actual-time gate the native parent density continues exponentially
while deposition is off. This review does not certify continuity, bound an
observable effect from the small residual parent fraction, derive the peer's
QED identity findings, or solve their consequences. Those findings and the
source/quadrature blockers justify stopping before a validated trajectory.
No finite-step h/h2 witness, transport trajectory, weak-rate history, BBN
abundance or observational prediction is claimed.
