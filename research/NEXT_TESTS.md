# Next bounded integration tests

These are prospective tests, not completed results or a retrospective preregistration. Constants and initial numerical outcomes of the present controls were known before this plan was written.

The [transport-source diagnostic round](rounds/2026-10-01-transport-source-audit/README.md) executes the pinned Nudec emitter and a local RHS with real collisions. The [Oct 2 source-adapter preflight](rounds/2026-10-02-source-adapter-preflight/README.md) adds an exact-muon-mass source callback, explicit native/frozen mass roles, canonical comoving-reference state/source mapping, actual pre-mixing callback capture and wrapper normalization and a half-open actual-time gate on fixed-state RHS fixtures. Neither round evolves spectra. Native primary-muon multiplicity remains `2*B_mumu`; the RHS already splits neutrino/antineutrino production by one half. Six exported species are declared charge-symmetric copies of three occupations, not six independent evolved histories.

The full reference-domain native-grid ladder still fails source moments at every tested resolution. The short-domain N4001/N8001 phase sweeps pass 1,001 sampled coordinates; this does not prove continuous-phase convergence or an adequate trajectory grid. Native immediate mixing also produces tau response, so a full mixed stepper cannot silently serve as a witness for the frozen pre-mixing source. Preserve these negatives, the earlier clipped-domain/endpoint-rounding controls and all wrapper/runtime receipts.

1. **Pressure-consistent EOS audit.** Before the first trajectory, specify a separately versioned treatment of plasma pressure, energy density and thermal-correction derivatives. Independently compare the temperature equation with derivatives of the declared EOS and include all source, parent and neutrino energy components. Distinguish fixed-state source bookkeeping from full energy/entropy continuity. Keep the frozen provider and releases unchanged; any corrected adapter variant needs its own code identity, assumptions and tests.
2. **Source-operator witness semantics.** Implement and instrument a genuine split pre-mixing source operator if the production stepper is to satisfy the frozen six-species pre-mixing response contract. Alternatively, review a separately versioned contract that explicitly accounts for the native immediately mixed source response. Retain the current frozen contract and observed mixed/pre-mixing source-response mismatch. A reconstructed Michel sidecar or relabeled mixed output cannot establish production consumption.
3. **Conservative grid and runtime budget.** Resolve full-history endpoint coverage, early source resolution and native quadrature conditioning before selecting a transport grid. Retain fixed positive uniform comoving nodes, native weights and a fresh JIT worker per configuration when claiming the native collision discretization. Nonuniform nodes, custom source/collision weights or a moving comoving grid require a separately justified conservative operator. Extend the successful sampled short-window source sweeps without treating them as transport validation; do not hide full-domain failures through renormalization.
4. **Matched zero/small-source transport.** After the preceding gates, introduce a separately specified unblinded small source, with the zero-source control first. Export every accepted step, actual pre-mixing source, evolved occupations, physical momentum map, parent/plasma/neutrino energy and pressure components, cutoff/event records and production-stepper witnesses. Check solver termination, the actual-time/domain gate and residual parent tail. Refine domain, momentum and timestep independently. Do not retune or use the blinded cards to find a desired answer.
5. **Threshold and endpoint adequacy.** Only after transport checks, split weak-rate quadrature at both charged-current landmarks and the beta-decay endpoint. Integrate through the returned physical transport domain, including the stopped-muon endpoint where applicable. Vary momentum resolution, upper boundary and time step; require independently stable absolute process rates. Thermal-tail convergence and equilibrium ratios alone are insufficient.
6. **Six-process semantics.** Keep the v0.9.20 archive fixed. Develop a separate reviewed contract that distinguishes inverse neutron decay from the two existing proton-to-neutron columns. Include the inverse process in the network total, or supply a quantitative negligible-contribution bound over the actual nonequilibrium history. Do not derive it from a common-temperature detailed-balance factor for injected spectra.
7. **Network consumption and control replay.** Confirm that the measured source-dependent rates are consumed by the nuclear network, with matched zero-source and energy-only controls. Record nuclear inputs, caches, configuration, raw evolution, and baryon/energy accounting. Establish the numerical precision budget before interpreting a pair contrast.
8. **Frozen pair only after integration checks.** Run both preserved scenarios at coarse and fine resolutions, validate complete returns, and keep a matched Standard Model control. Independent backend replication and physical review follow; successful software gates alone are not scientific confirmation.

The missing covariant microscopic/dark-energy model, abundance-generation mechanism, and structure/lensing calculations remain distinct open tasks. Additional fitted mass relations or analogy with another research program cannot replace these checks.
