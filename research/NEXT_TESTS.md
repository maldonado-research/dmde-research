# Next bounded integration tests

These are prospective tests, not completed results or a retrospective preregistration. Constants and initial numerical outcomes of the present controls were known before this plan was written.

1. **Unblinded source-to-weak-rate spike.** Introduce a separately specified small source into a momentum-resolved transport backend, with the zero-source control first. Export the actual source RHS, evolved electron-neutrino and antineutrino occupations, physical momentum mapping, background energy densities, and stepper witnesses. Do not retune or use the blinded cards to find a desired answer.
2. **Threshold and endpoint adequacy.** Split the rate quadrature at both charged-current landmarks and the beta-decay endpoint. Integrate through the returned physical transport domain, including the stopped-muon endpoint where applicable. Vary momentum resolution, upper boundary and time step; require independently stable absolute process rates. Thermal-tail convergence and equilibrium ratios alone are insufficient.
3. **Six-process semantics.** Keep the v0.9.20 archive fixed. Develop a separate reviewed contract that distinguishes inverse neutron decay from the two existing proton-to-neutron columns. Include the inverse process in the network total, or supply a quantitative negligible-contribution bound over the actual nonequilibrium history. Do not derive it from a common-temperature detailed-balance factor for injected spectra.
4. **Network consumption and control replay.** Confirm that the measured source-dependent rates are consumed by the nuclear network, with matched zero-source and energy-only controls. Record nuclear inputs, caches, configuration, raw evolution, and baryon/energy accounting. Establish the numerical precision budget before interpreting a pair contrast.
5. **Frozen pair only after integration checks.** Run both preserved scenarios at coarse and fine resolutions, validate complete returns, and keep a matched Standard Model control. Independent backend replication and physical review follow; successful software gates alone are not scientific confirmation.

The missing covariant microscopic/dark-energy model, abundance-generation mechanism, and structure/lensing calculations remain distinct open tasks. Additional fitted mass relations or analogy with another research program cannot replace these checks.

The [focused literature check](LITERATURE_CHECK.md) identifies nuDSMC at a pinned September 2026 commit as a candidate comparison route. Assess its documented source channels, neutrino/antineutrino symmetry, BBN handoff and runtime availability before selecting it. Its current native interface does not document primary muon decay or a Linux library, and the separate Python BBN project was not located. It does not supersede the executed PRIMAT control or establish an available DMDE adapter.
